03 / Problem-solving case study

On the Bench

A work-in-progress reflection on adapting to technical constraints during watershed mapping in Irisan.

The challenge

Working within technical limitations.

During data gathering for a watershed mapping project in Irisan, the laptop I was using struggled to handle the processing demands of the work. This created a practical challenge while collecting, organizing, and analyzing mapping data.

The project involved mapping, data collection, analysis, and presentation. I contributed across these stages, including work on the program and the handling of the data.

What this demonstrates

Resourcefulness in progress.

The final account of the workaround will be added once the specific steps are confirmed. This section will describe the actual adjustments made, rather than inventing technical procedures or results.
